The Programme

Train Like a Champion

Prince Boxing Malta is Prestige Group's elite boxing programme — designed for all levels, from first-timers stepping into the gym to seasoned fighters chasing titles.

Boxing is more than a sport. It's a discipline, a lifestyle, and a pathway to physical and mental excellence. Our coaches bring international experience to the Maltese ring.

  • Beginner to elite competition training
  • One-to-one personal coaching
  • Group classes and sparring sessions
  • Fitness and conditioning programmes
  • Youth boxing programme available
  • Professional event organisation
  • Tournament preparation and coaching
  • Nutrition and recovery guidance
