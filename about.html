Who We Are

Prestige Group Malta

Founded in 2026, Prestige Group was born from a vision to bring genuinely premium service to Malta — not just in name, but in every interaction, every response, and every result delivered.

We operate across two distinct verticals: professional towing and roadside recovery, and elite boxing training through our Prince Boxing Malta programme. Different industries, unified by the same relentless standard.

Malta deserves world-class services. We are here to deliver them — without compromise.
