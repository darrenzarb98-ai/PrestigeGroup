Who We Are

Prestige Group Malta

Founded in 2026, Prestige Group was born from a vision to bring genuinely premium service to Malta — not just in name, but in every interaction, every response, and every result delivered.

We operate across two distinct verticals: professional towing and roadside recovery, and elite boxing training through our Prince Boxing & Fitness Club programme. Different industries, unified by the same relentless standard.

Malta deserves world-class services. We are here to deliver them — without compromise.

The Founder

Started From Nothing

Jonathan Abdul Rehim started with nothing. No head start and no shortcuts — just the drive to build something of his own and the discipline to keep going when it was hard.

Before Prestige Group, Jonathan did a great deal of real estate, and that is where he learned the business side of building something that lasts.

Today he runs five companies across Malta and Gozo, all built on the same belief: work hard, treat people well, and never accept second best.
