Prestige Group Malta

Privacy & Data Terms

How we collect, use and protect your personal data, in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. Who we are

Prestige Group Malta (“we”, “us”) operates this website and its services: Prestige Towing, Prince Boxing & Fitness Club, OnSpot Mobile Wash and IT Services. We are the data controller for the personal data described here. You can reach us at info@prestigegroupmt.com or on +356 7956 0750.

2. What data we collect

  • Enquiries and bookings: your name, phone number, email address, vehicle or service details and the message you send us.
  • Members and boxers: your name, email, membership or programme details, and session attendance where you use an account.
  • Staff and coaches: your name, work login, and clock-in and clock-out times used to calculate working hours and pay.
  • Payments: payments are handled by Stripe. We receive confirmation of the payment, the amount, the product and your name or email, but we never see or store your full card number.
  • Referral codes: if you enter a referral code at checkout, we record it against your payment.
  • Technical data: basic information such as device type and pages visited, and a login session if you sign in.

3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To answer enquiries, take bookings and provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract).
  • To process payments, keep accounting records and meet tax obligations (legal obligation).
  • To run staff clock-in and pay (contract and legal obligation).
  • To keep the website and our accounts secure and to prevent fraud (legitimate interests).
  • To contact you about things you opted into, such as fight announcements (consent, which you can withdraw at any time).

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

4. Who we share it with

We only share data with the providers we need to run the service, and only for that purpose:

  • Supabase for secure database storage and logins.
  • Stripe for card and payment processing.
  • Vercel for hosting this website.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own data-protection terms. Some may process data outside the EU/EEA; where they do, this is covered by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

5. How we keep it safe

  • Logins are protected by encrypted passwords, and admin and staff areas are only open to signed-in users.
  • Access to data is limited by role: staff only see what they need for their work.
  • Connections to the website are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • We only collect the data we need, and we give staff accounts to named individuals only.

If a data breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the Maltese data-protection authority as the law requires.

6. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected, or as long as the law requires (for example, accounting and payroll records). Enquiry messages that did not lead to a booking are deleted when no longer needed. When data is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data (access),
  • correct data that is wrong,
  • delete your data (erasure), where the law allows,
  • limit or object to how we use it,
  • give you your data in a portable format,
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what we did before.

To use any of these rights, email info@prestigegroupmt.com. We will reply within one month. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDPC) in Malta at idpc.org.mt.

8. Staff, coaches and members

If you hold an account with us, you must keep your password private, never share it, and only use your own login to clock in or confirm attendance. Time and attendance records are used for pay and for managing session capacity. You can ask us to see or correct your own records at any time.

9. Children

Our services are not directed at children. If a person under 18 joins training, a parent or guardian should deal with us on their behalf, and we will only use their data for the purpose of providing the session.

10. Changes to this page

We may update these terms from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
